Shaman

As we know in Forever, there are quite a few changes with gearing and talents for Shaman.

However, we are still fulfilling the same role as in Classic, being a jack of all trades Support.

RACES

Tauren

Plainsrunning(not with flag) and War Stomp(now 1min30sec cd) are game-winners.

Plainsrunning, War Stomp, Endurance

Skyborne

Identical hitbox to TBC Belf. On demand 10% speed and 214% speed slow fall are great for movement.

Skysight, Walk on Air, Wind Blessed

Orc

Great defensive and offensive racials with Shatter Curse and Blood Fury.

Blood Fury, Hardiness, Command, Shatter Curse

Troll

You gain 20% health regen in combat, and a strong bandage that does not give bandage debuff. Amazing for self sustain. Beast Slaying works on Druid forms.

Berserking, Rapid Regeneration, Regeneration, Beast Slaying

Dwarf

Strong defensive racial, and very strong offensive racials. Allows you to move a point from hit talents elsewhere, and Big Game Hunter works on Druid forms.

Artisan's Precision, Stoneform, Big Game Hunter
